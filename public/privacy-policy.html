Privacy Policy

At Disposable Chat, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. No account creation is required to use our service.

2. Temporary Chat Data

All chat messages and voice notes are processed in real-time and self-destruct immediately after the chat session ends or when users leave the room.

3. Cookies & Advertising

We may use third-party advertising networks (like A-Ads) to display ads. These networks may use non-personal cookies to serve relevant advertisements.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to us via our home page.
