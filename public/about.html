About Disposable Chat

Disposable Chat is a free, web-based temporary messaging application built for users who value online privacy and speed.

Why Disposable Chat?

  • Zero Registration: No sign-up, no passwords, no email needed.
  • Self-Destructing: Chat messages vanish instantly when the room is terminated.
  • Multimedia Support: Send text messages and instant voice recordings seamlessly.
