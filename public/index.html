How it Works

Simply click "Create Room" to generate a unique 6-digit code. Share this code with your partner to start a private, end-to-end secure conversation. Once you exit, the chat room self-destructs forever.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this chat completely private?

Yes. We don't ask for any personal details. Your identity remains 100% hidden.

Are my messages saved?

No. All messages are deleted instantly once the chat session ends. We do not store your chat history.
